Xueyan Wang received his B.S. and M.S. degrees in Electrical Engineering from Xi’an University of Science and Technology in 2020 and 2023, respectively. He is currently a research assistant at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). His research interests include model predictive control of permanent magnet synchronous motor drives, power electronics, and power transmission.
