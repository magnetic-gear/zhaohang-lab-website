Pei Lu obtained her bachelor degree at Zhejiang University, and the MPhil degree at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). Currently, she is a Ph.D. student at Robotics and Autonomous Systems at HKUST(GZ), supervised by Prof. MA Shugen and co-supervised by Dr. ZHAO Hang. Her research focuses on configuration design and cooperative control of underwater robots, and actuator-level optimization.
