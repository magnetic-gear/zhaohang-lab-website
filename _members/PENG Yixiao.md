Yixiao Peng received the B.S. degree in energy and power engineering from the Tongji University, Shanghai, China, in 2018, and the M.S. degree in energy and power engineering from the Zhejiang University, Zhejiang, China, in 2023. He is currently working as a Research Assistant in the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), Guangzhou, China. His research interests include the multiphysics analysis and surrogate model of electric machines.
