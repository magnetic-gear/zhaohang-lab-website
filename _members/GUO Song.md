GUO Song graduated with a Bachelor of Engineering in Automation from the Department of Automation, School of Information Science and Engineering at Northeastern University, China. He then earned a Master of Philosophy (MPhil) in Robotics and Autonomous Systems from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou).

His research focuses on motor optimization design, motor control, and hardware design, aiming to enhance the performance and efficiency of electromechanical systems.
