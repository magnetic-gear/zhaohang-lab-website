Zhongyu Shi is currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Engineering Science at the University of Oxford. She is a visiting student at the HKUST(GZ). Her research interests focus on parameter identification of permanent magnet synchronous motors.
In her free time, she enjoys drawing and is a co-founder and flautist of the Oxford Chinese Orchestra.
