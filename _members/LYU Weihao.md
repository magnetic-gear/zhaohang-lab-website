I completed my M.Eng in Aircraft Design at the Aerospace Information Research Institute, Chinese Academy of Sciences. My research focused on the application of systems engineering principles to aerospace design, culminating in my thesis, "Research on Overall Optimization Design Method of Stratospheric Airship Based on MBSE." Prior to my graduate studies, I earned a B.Eng in Robot Engineering from the School of Electrical Engineering and Automation at Hefei University of Technology. Outside of my academic pursuits, I am a table tennis enthusiast.
